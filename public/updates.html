Version updates

What's new in your workspace, from small improvements to new ways to build your ideas.

The latest version is 0.5.0. Check the version shown in your app to see which build you're using. If an update is ready, choose Save and update to load it.

Version 0.5.0

Latest

October 17, 2026

Arrange your board toolbars

  • Move and resize. Drag the grip to position Board actions or Drawing tools. Drag the right edge to change its width, or use the width slider in ⋮ More. Keyboard arrow keys work on the grip and resize handle too.
  • A single row. Each bar keeps its controls and minimize button on one row. Extra controls are available in ⋮ More, including on smaller screens.
  • Your preferred order. Use ⋮ More → Arrange controls to move tools earlier or later. Show or hide button labels to fit more controls.
  • Remembered on this browser. Positions, widths, labels, and order persist across boards. Layouts stay within the board when screen size changes. Reset a toolbar from ⋮ More, or reset both layouts from the header’s Toolbars menu.
  • Clear editing space. The two floating bars stay out of the way while a mobile object palette, board menu, or editor dialog is open.

Earlier releases

v0.4.1Minimize each toolbar individually

October 17, 2026

Minimize each toolbar individually

  • A minus button on every toolbar. Minimize board actions, drawing, navigation, or editing directly on that toolbar.
  • Labeled restore tabs. Bring back a minimized toolbar from its tab on the board without opening the Toolbars menu.
  • Independent, remembered choices. Minimize one toolbar while keeping the others available. Choices stay on this browser after reopening a board.
  • Global controls remain available. Hide tools, Show tools, and the Toolbars menu still work in the header, including with the sidebar collapsed.
v0.4.0Icon stamps and visible toolbar controls

October 17, 2026

Icon stamps and visible toolbar controls

  • 34 icons for your notes. Mark clarification, reminders, research, ideas, tasks, warnings, and more. Search by icon name or meaning and choose a color and size.
  • Stamp with I. Press I to use your last icon, then tap or left-click the board. The icon is selected for resizing. Turn on Keep stamping for several placements; press Esc to stop.
  • See your selected icon. The board toolbar shows the current icon and name. Your icon, color, size, and repeat preference stay on this browser.
  • Clear hide and restore buttons. Hide tools and Show tools in the header minimize or restore all four floating toolbars. The labeled Toolbars menu lets you control them individually and remains visible with the sidebar hidden.
  • Local icon artwork. Stamps need no external image search or paid service, save with your board, and are included in backups and image sync.
v0.3.0More canvas space and faster diagrams

October 17, 2026

More canvas space and faster diagrams

  • A collapsible sidebar. Hide the left menu from the header and restore it when you need it.
  • Control your board toolbars. Show or hide board actions, drawing tools, navigation tools, and editing tools independently, or hide and restore them together. The header control stays accessible.
  • One-key diagram branches. Select a diagram shape or type its label, then press Tab to add a child or Shift+Tab to add a sibling. Your current label is saved, the new shape copies its preset, size, and style, and typing starts in its label.
  • Clearer creation dates. Older items keep an estimated creation date when an original date is unavailable. New items retain their creation date when edited, renamed, moved, imported, or synced.
  • A dedicated version history. Read the current and earlier release notes from About → Version updates, including without JavaScript.
v0.2.4Unlock objects & keep connector attachments

October 16, 2026

  • Use Unlock objects in the board controls to unlock a specific locked object or all locked objects, without selecting them on the canvas.
  • Unlock diagram labels and outlines together with their shape. Unlocking supports Undo.
  • Manually adjusted connector endpoints stay at the chosen point on a shape when it moves or resizes, including after reopening the board.
  • Preserve endpoint choices when changing connection routes. Use From/To side controls or drag an endpoint to deliberately change its attachment.
v0.2.3Restore diagram actions

October 16, 2026

  • Activate diagram actions when selecting objects in a saved board, even when its contents load after the editor starts.
  • Restore diagram actions on desktop when no mobile style panel is covering the canvas.
  • Keep Add child, Add sibling, Add connection, and Edit shape available inside the mobile Edit panel, without floating over its controls.
  • Return the actions beside the selected object when the Edit panel closes.
v0.2.2Simpler cloud settings

October 16, 2026

  • Cloud sync uses the connection configured for this Note deployment. Project URLs and public keys are no longer editable in Settings.
  • Ignore old connection overrides saved in the browser while keeping appearance, image storage, and OCR preferences.
v0.2.1Mobile diagram editing fix

October 16, 2026

  • Hide floating diagram actions while the built-in mobile Edit panel is open, so they no longer cover stroke, fill, text, and other style controls.
  • Restore the diagram actions when the Edit panel closes, without requiring a new selection.
v0.2.0Library organization & diagram editing

October 16, 2026

  • Visible app version with a link to this update history.
  • Separate New folder and New board controls in the library.
  • Independent folder and board sorting by name, creation date, or last modification, in either direction. Sort choices stay on this device.
  • Creation dates recorded for new items. Older items use their earliest available saved date.
  • Change a selected diagram shape from the palette while keeping its text and connections. Choose the shape for a child or sibling before adding it.
  • Canvas object controls stay behind the diagram palette on mobile.
