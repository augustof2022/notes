Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy describes the Note instance at augustof2022.github.io/notes/, maintained by the GitHub account augustof2022. Contact the operator at renz.r.salvador@gmail.com for privacy, support, or data-deletion requests.

Private from other users, accessible to the operator. Signed-in users have separate workspaces. The operator has administrative access to the Supabase database and storage and can access cloud notes, recognized text, and metadata stored there. Note does not provide end-to-end encryption.

1. Information Note accesses

  • Account information: when you sign in, the authentication service processes your account identifier, email address, and available profile information such as a name or profile image. With Google sign-in, Note requests openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile. Note does not receive your Google password or request Gmail access.
  • Workspace content: folder and board names, typed notes, drawings, handwriting strokes, board settings, pasted or attached images, and any recognized or reviewed searchable text.
  • Google Drive files: Note requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file to create, read, and manage files created by Note or explicitly authorized for the app. This is not permission to read every file in your Drive.
  • Browser data: locally saved boards and images, settings, authentication sessions, and cached app and recognition-model files.

2. How information is used

Note uses account information to sign you in and identify your workspace. It uses your content to display and edit boards, organize folders, search your notes, recognize text, export backups, and sync your workspace across devices. Drive access is used to save and retrieve your board images in a private “Note images” folder in your own account. Note does not make those files publicly shared.

Note does not sell personal data, show advertisements, or use workspace content to train machine-learning models. Image OCR and handwriting recognition run in your browser. Recognized text may sync with your workspace; your images and handwriting are not sent to a paid recognition service or to the model-download host for recognition.

3. Where information is stored

Note saves local boards and image copies in your browser’s IndexedDB and stores preferences and the Note authentication session in browser storage. Local-only workspaces do not sync to the Note database unless you sign in and copy them into an account.

Signed-in folders, boards, drawing data, image references, and searchable text sync to the operator’s Supabase project. Images are stored in your Google Drive when Drive is selected, or in the operator’s private Supabase Storage bucket when that option is selected. Changing storage affects new uploads; existing files retain their original storage.

Google Drive access tokens are held in memory by the app and are removed from the persisted browser session. The app does not persist Google refresh tokens. Your separate Note session can remain signed in until you sign out or clear browser storage.

4. Providers and access

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Supabase provides authentication, the database, and optional image storage. Google provides sign-in, Drive access, and web fonts used by the app. Optional handwriting recognition downloads a pretrained model from Hugging Face; the download host receives the model request, not your board content. These providers process requests under their own policies and may maintain connection, security, or operational logs.

Account ownership rules restrict normal Note users to their own records. Administrative privileges are different: the operator can read or modify cloud content in Supabase, including recognized text derived from images. Images stored in Supabase Storage are also administratively accessible. Owning the app does not itself give the operator access to your whole Drive or automatically share your Drive images with the operator’s personal Google account.

Human access to user content is limited to operating and securing the service, support with the user’s permission, or compliance with applicable law. Users should consider this access model when deciding what to store.

Note’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Retention and deletion

Local content remains until you remove or clear it. Cloud content remains while it is kept in your account; there is no automatic expiration schedule. Moving a board to Trash is recoverable and does not permanently delete its records or image files. Images deliberately remain in storage to support restoration, and storage cleanup is not automatic.

To request permanent deletion of your Note account or cloud workspace, email renz.r.salvador@gmail.com from the account address. The operator handles requests manually and may need to verify account ownership. Do not include passwords or sensitive board content in your request. Provider logs or any provider-maintained backup copies may have separate retention periods.

You control the image files in your own Drive and can remove them there. Removing Note account records does not automatically erase those Drive files, browser copies on other devices, or backups you exported. Export anything you want to keep before requesting deletion.

6. Your choices and security

You can use Note locally without signing in, choose the supported image-storage option, disable automatic image OCR, review handwriting text before saving it, export a backup, and sign out. Revoke Google access through your Google Account connections; this stops future authorized access but does not itself delete saved files or Supabase records.

Cloud requests use HTTPS and the app applies account access controls. These controls do not protect content from the service operator or guarantee that loss or unauthorized access is impossible. Signing out does not erase local copies; use a trusted device and manage browser data when sharing a device.

7. Updates and contact

Changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy and its update date. Contact renz.r.salvador@gmail.com with questions or requests.
